Kurani Finance Assistant

A private, read-only bookkeeping and reporting tool for Ravi Kurani's own companies.

Kurani Finance Assistant is an internal tool operated by Standard Water Corp. It reads QuickBooks Online data for Standard Water Corp, Poolify and Incodeks so their books and financial reports can be reviewed. It is not offered to the public.

Connecting and disconnecting

You connect the app by signing in through QuickBooks (Intuit) and approving access. You can disconnect at any time from the Apps section of your QuickBooks Online account. Disconnecting stops all further access.
