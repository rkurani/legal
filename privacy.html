Privacy Policy

Kurani Finance Assistant

Effective October 3, 2026.

Who we are

Kurani Finance Assistant (the "App") is a private, internal tool operated by Standard Water Corp ("SWC", "we", "us"). It is used only for companies owned or run by Ravi Kurani: Standard Water Corp, Poolify and Incodeks. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from the public.

What data the App accesses

When you connect a QuickBooks Online company, the App can read that company's accounting data, such as:

  • Company information and chart of accounts
  • Transactions, invoices, bills, payments and journal entries
  • Customer and vendor records
  • Financial reports such as profit and loss and balance sheet

Access is read-only. The App does not change your QuickBooks data and does not move money.

How we use it

We use this data only for bookkeeping, reconciliation and financial reporting for the companies listed above. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. It is processed only by the infrastructure and service providers needed to run the App, such as cloud hosting and the AI model providers that power the assistant (SpaceXAI, through Grok Bot, and Anthropic), under terms that do not let them use it for their own purposes. We may disclose data if required by law.

Security and storage

QuickBooks sign-in tokens are stored securely on infrastructure SWC controls, with access limited to authorized people. Data is sent over encrypted connections. We keep data only as long as it is needed for bookkeeping and reporting, or as required by law.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect the App at any time from the Apps section of your QuickBooks Online account. Disconnecting ends the App's access right away. To ask us to delete data the App has stored, email us and we will do so unless we are required by law to keep it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always posted at this address, with its effective date.
